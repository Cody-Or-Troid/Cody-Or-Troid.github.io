The last signal

The end of the world started silently. As silently as all storms do anyway, in the quiet of a November evening. There was no huge bang, or a fiery explosion, or even a great light that blinded society as a whole. No, instead the world was drowned in clouds and fog, thunder echoing from what sounded like space itself.
